Family Care Navigation questions

What is family care navigation?

Family care navigation is professional help for families managing a loved one's healthcare. A nurse practitioner reviews diagnoses, medications and instructions with you, identifies gaps, and gives you a written plan for next steps.

How is this different from a patient advocate or geriatric care manager?

Patient advocates usually focus on the system: appointments, bills and insurance. Geriatric care managers often handle housing and daily care. Family Care Navigation is led by a board-certified nurse practitioner, so it also covers the clinical side: what the medications, diagnoses and instructions actually mean.

What happens during the $129 consult?

On a secure video visit we review what's happening, the medications and providers involved, and any discharge or specialist instructions. You leave with priorities, questions for the care team, and a written Family Care Action Plan.

Can you help if my parent lives in another state?

Yes. Visits are virtual, so family members can join from anywhere. Clinical services depend on where your loved one lives; we currently serve [States served].

Is Family Care Navigation covered by insurance or Medicare?

[Confirm: Family Care Navigation is private-pay with transparent pricing. IHM accepts TRICARE East for eligible covered services from October 1, 2026.]

What should I have ready for the first visit?

Whatever you have: the medication list, discharge papers, recent visit summaries and a list of providers. If your loved one is an adult, they or their legal representative may need to give permission before health information is discussed.

Do you replace my loved one's doctors?

No. I work alongside the primary care provider, specialists, pharmacist and hospital team. Family Care Navigation adds education, organization and follow-through between visits.

Is this an emergency service?

No. For chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of stroke, severe bleeding or loss of consciousness, call 911 or seek emergency care right away.
