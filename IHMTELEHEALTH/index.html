Common questions

What is family care navigation?

Family care navigation is professional help for families managing a loved one's healthcare. A board-certified nurse practitioner reviews the diagnoses, medications and instructions with you, finds the gaps, and gives you a written plan for next steps.

How much does it cost?

Every family starts with a $129 Family Care Consult. If ongoing help would be useful, monthly navigation starts at $249 a month. We'll talk about it together, with no pressure.

Can you help if my parent lives in another state?

Yes. Visits are virtual, so long-distance caregivers can join from anywhere. Clinical services depend on where your loved one lives; we currently serve [States served].

Do you replace my parent's doctors?

No. I work alongside the existing care team, helping your family understand the plan, prepare questions and follow through. Family Care Navigation is not an emergency service; for an emergency, call 911.
