The Cost of Doing It All

Every hour spent marketing is an hour not spent building.

Most business owners didn't start their company to become a marketer. But somewhere between the invoices and the inbox, marketing becomes the thing that only happens when there's time left over — which is to say, rarely.

  • —Lead flow that swings between too much and not enough
  • —A brand message that shifts with whoever wrote it last
  • —Campaigns that react to slow weeks instead of preventing them
  • —No real time to test, measure, or improve any of it
